
## 1. ext4 anlegen und Eckdaten lesen
$ mkfs.ext4 -q -L daten-ext4 /dev/loop0
Filesystem volume name: daten-ext4
Filesystem features: has_journal ext_attr resize_inode dir_index filetype extent 64bit flex_bg sparse_super large_file huge_file dir_nlink extra_isize metadata_csum
Inode count: 16384
Block size: 4096

## 2. XFS anlegen und Eckdaten lesen
$ mkfs.xfs -q -L datenxfs /dev/loop1
meta-data=/dev/loop1 isize=512 agcount=4, agsize=19200 blks
data = bsize=4096 blocks=76800, imaxpct=25
naming =version 2 bsize=4096 ascii-ci=0, ftype=1
log =internal log bsize=4096 blocks=16384, version=2
